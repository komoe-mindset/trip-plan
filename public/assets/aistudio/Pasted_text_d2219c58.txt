Build a website that explains the business proposal to a Managing Director in simple Myanmar language, using a clear, step-by-step interactive presentation.

"Our company will explore opportunities to connect qualified Chinese AI technology providers with Myanmar businesses. ## STEP 6 — BUSINESS MODEL, BUDGET AND IMPLEMENTATION

Myanmar heading:

"ဒီလုပ်ငန်းက ဘယ်လိုဝင်ငွေရနိုင်မလဲ?"

First, explain possible revenue sources:

- One-time setup fees.
- Monthly AI Agent subscriptions.
- Custom business integration.
- Training and support services.
- Enterprise maintenance contracts.
- Approved reseller revenues, when authorised.

Create an interactive financial scenario calculator.

Inputs:

- Number of business customers.
- Monthly subscription per customer.
- Conversations per business per month.
- Model calls per conversation.
- Average input tokens per model call.
- Average output tokens per model call.
- Input API cost per 1 million tokens.
- Output API cost per 1 million tokens.
- Monthly hosting and other operating expenses.

Calculate:

Monthly revenue =
Business customers × Monthly subscription.

Total model calls =
Business customers × Conversations per business × Calls per conversation.

Total input tokens =
Total model calls × Input tokens per call.

Total output tokens =
Total model calls × Output tokens per call.

Estimated API cost =
(Input tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × Input rate)
+ (Output tokens ÷ 1,000,000 × Output rate).

Estimated monthly contribution =
Revenue − API cost − Other monthly operating expenses.

Add a clear disclaimer:

"ဤတွက်ချက်မှုသည် စီမံကိန်းအတွက် ခန့်မှန်းတွက်ချက်မှုသာဖြစ်ပြီး အမှန်တကယ်အမြတ်ငွေကို အာမခံခြင်း မဟုတ်ပါ။"

Use editable assumptions and clearly identify any sample values.

Explain that salaries, taxes, customer acquisition, support, security, and other costs may not be fully represented.
